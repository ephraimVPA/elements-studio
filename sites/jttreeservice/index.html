What we do

Tree Work, Start To Finish

If it involves a tree on your property, we handle it. Residential and commercial, across the Toledo area.

Tree Removal

Full takedowns, including tight-access trees near houses, garages, and power lines. Rigged down in sections when there is no room to drop it.

Trimming And Pruning

Shaping, deadwood removal, and clearing limbs off roofs and driveways. Cuts made where they help the tree, not just where they are easy.

Stump Grinding

Lot And Brush Clearing

Cleanup And Haul Away
