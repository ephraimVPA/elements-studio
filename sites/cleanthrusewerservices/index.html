What we do

Drain and sewer work, done right the first time

From a slow kitchen sink to a main line that keeps backing up, here is how we help homes and small businesses in the Perrysburg area.

Drain cleaning

Sinks, tubs, toilets and floor drains cleared with the right tool for the clog, not a quick snake that comes back next week.

Main sewer lines

Repeated backups usually mean a problem out in the main line. We find it and clear it so the whole house drains again.

Hydro jetting

High-pressure water that scours grease and roots out of the pipe wall, so the line runs full-size instead of barely open.

Camera inspection

We send a camera down the line and show you what we see, so you are deciding on facts and not a guess.

Sump pumps

Pump testing, repair and replacement so your basement stays dry through the next heavy Ohio rain.

Repairs and replacement

When a pipe is broken or collapsed, we fix or replace the bad section and leave the rest of your line alone.
