What we do

Full service auto repair, under one roof

From a check-engine light to brakes and everything in between. If it drives, we can look at it.

Engine diagnostics

Check-engine lights, rough idle, stalling. We read the codes and find the real cause before we quote you.

Brakes and rotors

Squealing, grinding, or a soft pedal. New pads, rotors, and lines so you stop the way you should.

Oil and maintenance

Oil changes, fluids, filters, and belts on schedule so small jobs never turn into big ones.

Tires and alignment

Mounting, balancing, rotations, and alignments to keep the car tracking straight and tires lasting.

Heating and AC

No heat in January or no cold air in July. We test the system and fix what is actually leaking.

Batteries and starting

Slow cranks and dead batteries. We test the battery, alternator, and starter and get you turning over again.
