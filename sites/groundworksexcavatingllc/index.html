What we do

Dirt work, done by people who answer the phone.

From the first cut to the final grade, Ground Works handles the heavy, messy part of your project so the rest goes smooth.

Grading & Leveling

Rough and finish grading for driveways, pads, and yards so water runs where it should and nothing settles wrong.

Drainage & French Drains

Wet basement or a yard that holds water? We trench, pipe, and backfill to pull water away from the house for good.

Foundations & Footers

Digging for additions, garages, and new builds. Square, to depth, and ready for the concrete crew.

Demolition & Haul-Off

Old slabs, sheds, and foundations taken down and carried off. We leave the site clean and ready for what comes next.

Site Prep & Clearing

Brush, stumps, and topsoil stripped and staged so your pole barn, pool, or addition starts on solid ground.

Gravel & Driveways

New stone driveways and lots, shaped and compacted. Culverts set, approaches cut, ruts and potholes filled.
