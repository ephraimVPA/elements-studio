What we do

Collision and auto body work, start to finish

From a parking-lot dent to a real collision, we straighten it, refinish it and match the color so the repair does not stand out.

Collision repair

We straighten frames and replace panels after a wreck, then check that everything lines up the way the factory built it.

Dent & scratch repair

Door dings, parking-lot dents and keyed panels smoothed out and repainted so the fix disappears.

Color-matched paint

We scan your paint code and blend the refinish into the panels around it, so you cannot pick out the repair.

Bumper & panel replacement

Cracked bumpers and bent panels swapped for the right parts and painted to match before they go on.

Frame & alignment checks

After a hit we measure the frame and suspension points so the car tracks straight and the tires wear even.

Insurance estimates

Bring the claim number. We write the estimate and work with your adjuster so you are not stuck in the middle.
