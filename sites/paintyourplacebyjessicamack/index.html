What we paint

Interior and exterior work, prepped and finished with care

From a single room refresh to the whole exterior, the prep gets the same attention as the paint. That is what makes it last.

Interior rooms

Walls, ceilings and trim rolled and cut clean, with furniture covered and floors protected start to finish.

Exterior & siding

Siding, soffits and doors washed, scraped and coated so the color holds up through Toledo winters.

Cabinets & trim

Kitchen cabinets and woodwork sanded, primed and sprayed for a smooth finish that looks factory made.

Prep & repair

Cracks, dings and old peeling patched and sanded first, because good paint over bad prep never lasts.

Color help

Not sure on the shade? We help you test colors on the wall before you commit to the whole room.

Clean handover

Tape pulled, floors cleared and everything put back, so you walk into a finished room, not a job site.

How it works
