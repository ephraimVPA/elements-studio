What we do

Roofing and siding for Maumee homes

New roofs, repairs, siding and the storm work that comes with Ohio weather. One crew that does the whole exterior.

Roof replacement

A full tear-off and new roof when the old one is past patching, done with quality shingles and clean flashing.

Roof repair

Leaks, missing shingles and worn spots fixed before they turn into water damage inside the house.

Siding

New siding that protects the walls and freshens the whole look of the house, installed to shed water the right way.

Gutters

Continuous gutters and downspouts that carry water away from the roof line and the foundation.

Storm damage

Wind and hail take a toll on a roof. We inspect the damage and walk you through what the repair involves.

Low-slope roofs

Flat and low-slope sections sealed with the right membrane so standing water does not find a way in.
