What we do

Garage door repair and installation, handled by one crew

Most repairs come down to a few worn parts. We carry them on the truck so you are not waiting days for a door that will not open.

Broken springs

Torsion and extension springs replaced with the right rating for your door, so it lifts smooth and balanced.

Off-track doors

Rollers and cables realigned, bent tracks straightened or swapped, so the door stops binding and grinding.

Opener repair

Motors, gears, sensors and remotes diagnosed and fixed, or a quiet new opener installed if yours is done.

Cables & rollers

Frayed cables and worn rollers are cheap parts that cause big problems. We replace them before they strand you.

New door installs

Steel, insulated and carriage-style doors measured, ordered and hung, with the old one hauled away.

Safety tune-ups

A yearly check on balance, sensors and hardware keeps a heavy door from becoming a hazard.
